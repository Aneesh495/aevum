# Shock error and chronology

A pandemic indicator is allowed to change fitted 2020 and 2021 rates only after their event years enter the training window. The retrospective modeled release was unavailable in those years, so temporal separation alone does not imply historical access. The historical-vintage comparison uses a separate source edition.

For one-year WPP 2024 endpoints, the coherent factor's mean absolute e0 error is 1.14 years during 2020–2021 and 0.40 in other years. Its 80% coverage drops from 74.6% to 31.0%. The WHO registration composition analysis finds a simultaneous rise in share error, but the different cause source and denominator prevent treating both as a single outcome series. A common shock affects multiple countries and limits independent replication. [Shock strata](figures/strata.csv)
