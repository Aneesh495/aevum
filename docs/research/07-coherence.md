# Coherence and horizon

A same-sex reference factor supplies the common future drift. Country log-rate differences retain a fitted age profile and a stationary autoregressive term, with persistence bounded below one. This specification makes unsupported divergent changes in country rate ratios decay. It also assumes that an idiosyncratic country shift will not persist unboundedly.

The coherent factor leads the one-year rolling WPP comparison with WIS 0.343 years across 42 countries and both sexes. At twenty years, its WIS is 2.006, compared with 1.669 for the age-specific count trend. Common drift is a defensible short-horizon regularizer in this experiment. Mean reversion itself is an assumption and does not guarantee long-horizon gain. [Score figure](figures/forecast-wis.svg) · [Horizon strata](figures/strata.csv)
