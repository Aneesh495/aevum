# Calibration of expectancy paths

The same sampled calendar blocks drive all ages and populations within each path. This retains some correlated shocks across age and geography. Perturbations to recent counts and exposure act on the endpoint rate level. Propagating entire rate paths through expectancy preserves the nonlinear contribution of survival and the open interval. The 2% exposure term is a declared sensitivity, with source revisions and model-form uncertainty omitted.

For the coherent baseline in the 42-country WPP 2024 frame, 80% interval width rises from 1.15 years at one year to 2.95 at twenty. Coverage drops from 67.9% to 25.7%. Age-space correlations and honest cutoff enforcement are insufficient when future systematic drift falls outside the empirical residual path.
